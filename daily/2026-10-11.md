**错误要点**
1. **过去时用错**：叙述已发生的事应统一用过去时。Today is sunshine → It was sunny；we eat → we ate；it's really nice → it was really nice。
2. **不规则动词与拼写**：eat→ate、buy→bought（aet、bougth）；planed→planned；satisfaired→satisfied；deliours→delicious；Beacuse→Because；travler→traveller；lingera→lingerie；vetam→Vietnam；chessburger→cheeseburger；coffe→coffee；back→black；eatting→eating。
3. **大小写**：专有名词和句首要大写（Brisbane、Kadek、Rednote、Costco、Inala Plaza、After）。
4. **主语/代词**：I and Kadek → Kadek and I；me bought → I bought；me nothing → I didn't buy anything。
5. **词性与介词/冠词**：sunshine（名词）→ sunny（形容词）；arrived brisbane → arrived in Brisbane；go fish market → go to the fish market；at there → there；in here → here。
6. **句型**：there has → there is；After finish → After we finished；talked about each other what → talked about what we did；which located at → which is located at。
7. **标点**：多处用句号把一个完整句子切断，且句号后小写开头（After finish our breakfast. we were...）。

**批改时的猜测（需确认）**：took a cup of coffee at home → 我猜是"带走一杯咖啡（to take away）"；fries pork cancake → 我猜是"炸猪肉饼（fried pork pancakes）"。

**评价与亮点（2026-10-11）**
1. **敢写、写得长**：第一天就写了约 400 词，把一整天从早到晚写完，不卡壳。愿意写长是最宝贵的习惯。
2. **叙事结构清楚**：昨天约朋友 → 今天 8:30 出发 → 早餐 → Mount Coot-tha → Inala Plaza → 午饭 → Costco → 回家，时间线不乱。这是雅思口语 Part 2 讲故事需要的能力。
3. **细节具体**：时间、价格、点了什么、找了多久、吃了几盘，画面感强，不空洞。
4. **会解释原因**：用 so、because、since 讲清为什么去某地、为什么人少，说明思路成体系，是写作拿高分的核心能力。
5. **表达地道有语气**：I stopped myself、we kept looking … finally found it、To be honest、Yeah, we talk about stuff like this。
6. **有自己的声音**：写得诚实、直接，有个人真实感。
7. **错误偏"粗心型"，不是"不会型"**：过去时没统一、大小写、拼写（如 aet）都是学过或一看就懂的，写完检查一遍就能少一半，提升空间大。
