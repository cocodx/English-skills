## 阅读复述：三篇 BBC 新闻

**我的原句**
Police said that a 16-year-old former student shooted dead one theacher and 4 students at an elementary school in Oregon.
A teacher and a bystander stopped him.
Ukraine says that its counter-offensive, Operation succeed, has push Russia back 277 square kilometres in eastern Ukraine.
But Ukraine is worried because StartLink's internet was restricted without warning.
Katie Zacharia is leaving her job as White House press secretary after an interview.
She was criticised because she didn't get any credentials.

**修改版**
> Police said that a 16-year-old former student shot dead a teacher and three children at an elementary school in Oregon.
> A teacher and a bystander stopped him.
>
> Ukraine says that its counter-offensive, Operation Vivaldi, has liberated 277 square kilometres in eastern Ukraine.
> But Ukraine is worried because Starlink was restricted without warning.
>
> Katie Zacharia is leaving her job as White House press secretary after less than five days.
> She was criticised for her first press briefing and her legal credentials.

**错误要点**
1. **shooted → shot**：不规则动词 shoot–shot–shot；*shot dead* 是"开枪打死"。
2. **人数与原文不符**：原文是三名学生加一名老师遇难（共四名），不是"一名老师加四名学生"。→ *a teacher and three children*。
3. **theacher → teacher**：拼写。
4. **Operation succeed → Operation Vivaldi**：Vivaldi 是行动的名字（原文说名字取自维瓦尔第的协奏曲）。
5. **has push → has pushed**：这是你第一次主动用**现在完成时**（has + 过去分词），结构对了，只差动词变成过去分词 pushed。
6. **pushed Russia back 277 square kilometres**：277 平方公里是**面积**，不是后退的距离。原文是收复了 277 平方公里（liberated 277 sq km）。
7. **StartLink → Starlink**：拼写。
8. **after an interview → after less than five days**：原文标题说她上任不到五天就离职，不是因为一次采访。
9. **she didn't get any credentials**：说得太绝对。原文是她被追问法律资历，承认自己没有通过律师资格考试，但她说自己有在中国从事国际交易法的"相应资质"。复述时不要说得比原文更绝对（和 10-14 的 *AI is conscious* 是同一类问题）。

**亮点**
- 6 句都写出来了，骨架填得基本准确，A teacher and a bystander stopped him 概括得很好。
- Police said that / Ukraine says that 转述用对了。
- **被动语态用对了两次**：*was restricted*、*was criticised*（之前常错，比如 was found install）。
- 第一次尝试现在完成时（has push），方向正确。
- 大小写（Oregon、Ukraine、Russia、White House）全对，criticised、credentials、bystander 等词也拼对了。

## 听写（Dictation）：Coach Shane · Daily English Dictation 第 3 集

**我听到的**
There are 3 things that never discuss with people religon politics and great pumpkin

**原文**
> There are three things I have learned never to discuss with people: religion, politics, and the Great Pumpkin.

**听对的部分**：There are three things、discuss with people、politics、pumpkin。内容词几乎全对。

**错误分析**
1. **漏听 I have learned**：*I have learned* 读得又轻又快（接近 "I've learned"），被听成了 that。类型：弱读。（10-15 听写时 *I have* 也被听成了 "a"，这是第二次）
2. **漏听 to**：*never to discuss* 里的 to 读成很轻的 /tə/，没听出来。类型：弱读。
3. **漏听 the**：*and the Great Pumpkin* 里的 the 读得很轻，没听出来。类型：弱读。
4. **religon → religion**：拼写，结尾是 -ion。
5. **标点和大小写**：people 后面有冒号，三个并列项之间有逗号；*Great Pumpkin* 是名字，两个词首字母都要大写。

**规律**
- 内容词（things、discuss、religion、politics、pumpkin）基本都听出来了，漏掉的是**小词**（I have、to、the）。小词在英语里本来就读得又轻又快。
- **自查办法**：写完读一遍，如果句子不通顺，那里往往就是漏听的地方。*things that never discuss with people* 缺少主语，说明漏了东西。
- **和口语的联系**：你自己说话时，也要把这些小词读轻、读快，内容词读重，这样才有"腔调"。

**说明**：这句话出自漫画 Peanuts（《花生漫画》/史努比）里的 Linus，Great Pumpkin 是他相信的一个虚构人物，所以要大写。

## 写作（日记）
> 约 250 词，写昨天下班去 Costco 的经过，以及路上和朋友 Keller 发生的不愉快。

**原文**
Yesterday, during the lunch time I heard the collgues talking about going to Csocto at satarday. It reminded me to get some food there for next week. So after finishing time I drived my car to there by myself. When I was driving, my friend called keller who sent me a message asking me go to his place on tomorrow or never see him. I replied me I don't want to go there immediately, Maybe the noise of the wind and other driving conditions. He couldn't hear anything from the voice message. He sent me he can't hear anything. **修改版**
Yesterday, during lunch, I heard my colleagues talking about going to Costco on Saturday. It reminded me to get some food there for next week, so after work I drove there by myself.

While I was driving, my friend Keller sent me a message asking me to go to his place tomorrow, or I would never see him again. I replied with a voice message saying I didn't want to go right away, but maybe because of the noise of the wind and the driving conditions, he couldn't hear anything. He texted me that he couldn't hear anything.

When I arrived at the supermarket, I thanked him for inviting me to his home, but I said I just wanted to stay at home. He got angry and said, "I asked you so many times, but you never come to see me. OK, that's fine. We will never meet again." I felt uncomfortable because I don't like being forced to do things I don't want to do.

I bought a lot of groceries, including beef, chips, potatoes, garlic, roast chicken, bread rolls, carrots and onions. It cost me nearly 127 Australian dollars.

**错误要点**
1. **拼写**：collgues → colleagues；Csocto → Costco；satarday → Saturday；potatos → potatoes；galic → garlic；rostaed → roasted；oninons → onions；dollors → dollars。
2. **不规则动词**：drived → drove（drive–drove–driven）。*drove my car to there* → *drove there*（there 前面不加 to）。
3. **介词和冠词**：at Saturday → on Saturday；on tomorrow → tomorrow（tomorrow 前不加介词）；the lunch time → lunch；arrived the supermarket → arrived at the supermarket。
4. **转述过去的话，时态要往回退**：I don't want → I didn't want；he can't hear → he couldn't hear；It costs me → It cost me。
5. **动词后面的结构**：asking me go → asking me **to** go；I replied me ... → I replied with a voice message / I replied that ...；He sent me he can't hear → He texted me that he couldn't hear。
6. **连接词和搭配**：Since I arrived → When I arrived（since 表示"因为"，不表示"当……时"）；expressed the gratitude of being inviting his home → thanked him for inviting me to his home；he was angry that he said → he got angry and said；daily groceries includes → groceries, including；small breads → bread rolls（bread 不可数）。
7. **被动**：I dislike the forcing of me doing → I don't like being forced to do。
8. **大小写和标点**：keller → Keller；句中的 Maybe、Ok → maybe、OK；*I just want to stay at home, he was angry ...* 用逗号把两个句子连在一起，要用句号或连词隔开。

**批改时的猜测（需确认）**：
- *asking me go to his place on tomorrow or never see him* → 我猜是"要么明天去他家，要么以后再也不见面"，改成 *or I would never see him again*。
- *I replied me I don't want to go there immediately* → 我猜是你回了一条语音，说不想马上过去；后面因为风噪他没听清。如果意思不对，告诉我。

**评价与亮点**
1. **叙事完整**：想去 Costco → 路上收到信息 → 产生误会和冲突 → 买东西，起承转合清楚。
2. **地道表达用对了**：*It reminded me to get some food there*（remind sb to do）、*by myself*、*nearly 127 Australian dollars*。
3. **过去进行时用对了**：*When I was driving*，没有错。
4. **有观点加理由**：*I felt uncomfortable because I don't like being forced ...*，这个"感受 + 原因"的结构你已经稳定在用。
5. **敢尝试复杂结构**：*the forcing of me doing*（把"被强迫"名词化），虽然不对，但这是很高级的尝试，改成 *being forced to do* 就对了。
6. **生活信息具体**：买了什么、花了多少，一个一个列出来。

**重点提醒**：这篇最大的问题是**转述时态没往回退**和**拼写**。写完先自查两项：过去的事里说的话，是否也用了过去时（didn't want、couldn't hear）？拼写有没有手滑？
