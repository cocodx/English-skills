## 1. 今日阅读
- **文章**：How is the US economy really doing under President Trump?（BBC）
- **话题**：经济增长、通胀、家庭收入、就业、借贷成本
- **文章结构**：好的一面（GDP 增长）+ 不好的一面（物价上涨、收入增长受拖累、就业放缓、借贷成本上升）

## 2. 今日词汇 / 搭配

| 搭配 | 意思 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| push up prices / inflation | 推高物价 / 通胀 | Higher energy costs have pushed up the cost of living. |
| hold back growth | 拖累增长 | Weak consumer spending has held back economic growth. |
| be better off / worse off | 境况更好 / 更差 | Many low-income families are worse off than a year ago. |
| put upward pressure on... | 给……带来上涨压力 | Higher interest rates put upward pressure on mortgage payments. |
| be down on... | 比……低（多用于对比） | Job growth is well down on last year. |
| grow at a steady pace | 稳步增长 | The economy has grown at a steady pace. |

**话题词**：GDP、inflation、tariff、budget deficit、borrowing costs、unemployment rate、household income、mortgage、labour force

## 3. 今日语法点
- **Although 与 However 不能同时使用**：although 本身已表示转折，不能再加 however。
  - 一句话：Although A, B.
  - 两句话：A. However, B.

## 4. 今日写作

**我的原句**
> Although the economy has grown at a steady pace, However, the war has pushed up inflation, and many households are worse off.

**修改后（二选一）**
> Although the US economy has grown at a steady pace, the war has pushed up inflation, and many households are worse off.

> The US economy has grown at a steady pace. However, the war has pushed up inflation, and many households are worse off.

**今日错误记录**
1. Although 和 However 重复使用（受中文"虽然……但是……"影响）
2. 拼写：wrose → worse
3. 指代不明：the economy → the US economy

## 4.5 第二篇阅读：UK to deploy jets to defend Saudi Arabia from Houthi attacks（BBC）
- **话题**：国际冲突、军事防御、能源供应

| 搭配 | 意思 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| in response to... | 作为对……的回应 | The government increased security in response to the threat. |
| be locked in a conflict / dispute | 陷入冲突、僵局 | The two sides have been locked in a dispute for years. |
| be backed by... | 得到……的支持 | The new policy is backed by most voters. |
| disrupt supplies | 扰乱供应 | Severe storms have disrupted food supplies. |
| vow to do sth | 誓言要做某事 | The company vowed to improve its safety record. |
| escalate the situation | 使局势升级 | Harsh words may only escalate the situation. |

**话题词**：defence、deploy、attack、civil war、infrastructure、energy supplies、casualties、military support、statement

**语法点**：新闻用现在完成时引出最新消息（has said / has announced）；写作转述观点时可用 According to... 或 X has argued that...，比反复用 say 更丰富。

**我的写作句子（无语法错误）**
> The UK will deploy jets to defend Saudi Arabia in response to Houthi attacks.

**可提升版本**
> The UK will deploy RAF Typhoon jets to defend Saudi Arabia in response to escalating Houthi attacks.

**提升点**：加具体名词（RAF Typhoon jets）和精准形容词（escalating）。
