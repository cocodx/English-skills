## 写作（复述）
**我的原句**
Zelensky said that Ukraine will resume strikes on Russian oil refineries, because he thinks the US only limited Ukraine's action not as well Russia

**修改版**
> Zelensky said that Ukraine will resume strikes on Russian oil refineries, because he thinks the US only restricted Ukraine's strikes, but not Russia's.

**错误要点**
1. **limited Ukraine's action → restricted Ukraine's strikes**：action 太泛，指攻击要用 strikes / attacks；restrict 比 limit 更适合"限制某人的行动"。
2. **not as well Russia → but not Russia's**：not as well 不是固定搭配。表示"只限制了 A，没限制 B"，用 *but not B* 或 *and not B*。（as well 是"也"，放在句末，如 *Russia as well*。）
3. 句末缺标点：整句要以句号结束。

**亮点**
- 抓到了核心因果：乌克兰被要求克制，俄罗斯却没有被限制。
- 用对了今天的新词 resume（还是第一次主动用）。
- 所有格 Ukraine's 用对了（之前常漏所有格），Zelensky、Russian、US 的大写全部正确，拼写全部正确。
- 开头 Zelensky said that ... 转述结构稳，没有把别人的话当成自己的结论。

## 第二篇：关于"AI 是否有意识"的争论（分析类长文）

### 文章内容
文章讲的是科技界对"AI 可能有意识吗、要不要考虑 AI 福利"的争论：一些人认为存在这种可能并主张采取谨慎措施，另一些专家认为这很荒谬，甚至会带来风险。文中引用了多位专家的不同观点，没有统一结论。

### 写作（复述）
**我的原句**
Some experts think that AI is conscious, but others believe that is ridicouls.

**修改版**
> Some experts think that AI could be conscious, but others believe that the idea is ridiculous.

**错误要点**
1. **ridicouls → ridiculous**：拼写错误，而且 ridiculous 是形容词，后面不加 s。读音是 ri-DIC-u-lous，四个音节。
2. **others believe that is ridiculous → others believe that the idea is ridiculous**：believe that 后面要有完整的主语和谓语，缺了"这个想法"。也可以写 *others think it is ridiculous*。
3. **精确度（可选）**：文章讲的是"有可能有意识"，不是"已经有意识"。*AI is conscious* 说得太肯定，改成 *AI could be conscious* 更准确。

**亮点**
- **Some ..., but others ...** 这个对比结构用得很对，正是雅思 Task 2 最常用的句式（Some people argue that ..., while others believe that ...）。
- 抓住了整篇文章的核心：专家们的看法不一致。
- 时态正确，AI 的大写正确，只有 1 个拼写错误。

## 听写（Dictation）：Coach Shane · Daily English Dictation 第 2 集（p=2）

**我听到的**
Sir, what time do you have? a five clock.

**原文**
> Jerry, what time do you have?
> I have 5 o'clock.

**听对的部分**：*what time do you have?* 完整正确。

**错误分析**
1. **Sir → Jerry**：句首的人名没听出来。类型：句首第一个词 + 人名（没预期是名字）。这是第二次句首第一个词听错（10-14 是 other → Mother）。
2. **a → I have**：*I have* 读得又快又轻，听起来只剩一个轻轻的 "a"。类型：弱读。
3. **five clock → 5 o'clock**：o'clock 是 *of the clock* 的缩写，读音接近 /əˈklɒk/，前面的 o' 读得很轻，所以听成 clock。类型：弱读。
4. **a 首字母小写**：问号后面是新句子，要大写（I have）。类型：大小写。

**说明**：*I have 5 o'clock* 母语者一般不这样说，自然的说法是 *It's five o'clock.*，听写以视频原文为准，但自己说话时不要照搬。

**规律**：句首第一个词（Mother、Jerry）两次都没听对。对策：听写时先放一放句首，听完整句再回头猜；有逗号在后面，往往前面是称呼或人名。

**修改版**
Today was a rainy and very cold day, so I just stayed at home after work. I read two BBC news articles patiently and did a dictation. I heard "Sir", but the answer was "Jerry".

I felt directionless and disappointed because I recently heard some bad news from China. It was about the Chinese government. I felt like they don't care about the poor people in China. An official surnamed Li told the media that they needed to get as many people as possible to pay social insurance (社保), but you know, life in China is very tough, because I lived in China before I came to Australia. At that time I felt life was meaningless and I had no hope. I couldn't find a way to reach the middle class, with an apartment and a car. They just want money, but I have never heard that they force companies to abide by the labour law, which says workers should only work eight hours a day. That news made me feel down.

**错误要点**
1. **不可数名词**：news 不可数，不能说 2 BBC news 或 a bad news。→ two BBC news articles；some bad news。
2. **拼写和词形**：raining day → rainy day（形容天气用 rainy）；disappionted → disappointed；cause → because；labourer law → labour law。
3. **大小写**：the chinese government → the Chinese government。
4. **时态**：It's about the government → It was about ...；felt life is meaningless → felt life was meaningless（过去的感受用过去时）。
5. **搭配和用词**：made me down → made me feel down；abide the law → abide by the law；8 hours one day → eight hours a day；the lives in China is → life in China is；from China domestic → from China。
6. **转述断裂**：*the big guy called Li told the media, we have to all people as many as we can to pay ...* 是几个意思拼在一起的，转述别人的话要用 *told the media that + 完整句子*（told the media that they needed to get as many people as possible to pay ...）。

**批改时的猜测（需确认）**：
- *a media level* → 我猜是 *the middle class*（中产），media 可能是 middle 的手滑。
- *社保* → 我写成了 social insurance（社保）。

**评价与亮点**
1. **能写真实的想法和理由**：*I felt ... because ...*、*I felt like they don't care ...* 是"观点 + 理由"的结构，对雅思写作和口语都很有用。
2. **用词亮眼**：directionless 这个词用得很准确；abide（缺 by）、patiently 也都用得对。
3. **有"过去和现在"的对比**：*I lived in China before I came to Australia*，before 后面接过去时，用对了。
4. **把今天的学习写进日记**：把听写里 Sir/Jerry 的小插曲也写了进来，说明你在把学习和生活连起来。
5. **用中文占位 社保**：没有被一个词卡住整句。
